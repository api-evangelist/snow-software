Application packaging and deployment remain complex and time-consuming tasks for enterprise IT teams. From understanding installer behavior to managing custom scripts and ensuring compatibility, packaging often involves manual effort, deep expertise, and constant troubleshooting. To address these challenges, AdminStudio is evolving with AI-driven capabilities that transform packaging workflows into a more insight-driven and automation-assisted experience, helping teams move faster, reduce risk, and preserve critical knowledge.
